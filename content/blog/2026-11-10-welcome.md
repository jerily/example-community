# A place to build together

The gate is green, the kettle works, and Juniper Yard is open.

Six months ago this was a patch of cracked concrete behind the railway arches.
Now there are four raised beds, a workbench made from an old door, and a surprising
number of people with opinions about compost. We wouldn’t change a thing.

## Bring what you have

You don’t need to be a gardener or know your way around a toolbox. Bring a question,
a spare hour, a packet of seeds, or just yourself. The repair table meets on Saturday
mornings; the garden crew is here on Sundays. There’s always a mug waiting.

[Introduce yourself in the forum](/forum), or browse the [community handbook](/wiki/community-handbook).
This is the first page of a story we get to write together.
