We’re the neighbors turning an old railway yard into a garden, a workshop, and a reason to say hello. Bring a little curiosity. We’ll put the kettle on.

## Pull up a chair.

A borrowed spade. A repaired lamp. A conversation that becomes a good idea. There’s room for all of it here — and for you, whether you’ve lived in Alderwick for years or just unpacked the last box.

- **Around the table** — Ask a question, offer a hand, or find someone who knows a thing you don’t. [Join a conversation →](/forum)
- **The shared notebook** — Planting notes, workshop guides, and the useful things we learn along the way. [Open the handbook →](/wiki/community-handbook)
- **Letters from the yard** — Small victories, muddy boots, and stories from our little corner of town. [Read the journal →](/blog)

## There’s always something on.

No experience needed. No perfect tools required. Just turn up at the green gate on Bramble Lane.

| When | What’s happening | Where to find us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saturday · 10–12 | **The repair table** — bring something that needs a second chance | The old potting shed |
| Sunday · 9–11 | **Good morning, garden** — a little weeding, a lot of tea | The raised beds |
| First Thursday · 6 pm | **Soup & small ideas** — a shared meal and a plan or two | Under the courtyard lights |

## Start small. Make yourself at home.

Say hello in the forum, tell us what you’re curious about, or add one useful thing to the notebook. Being a member means having a voice, not having all the answers.
