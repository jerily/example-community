# Community handbook

Welcome to Juniper Yard, our fictional neighborhood garden and workshop in Alderwick.
This notebook belongs to everyone who stops by. Sign in to add what you know.

## Your first visit

Find the green gate on Bramble Lane. The kettle lives in the old potting shed;
the mugs are mismatched, and that’s how we like them. Introduce yourself to whoever
is nearby, or [say hello in the forum](/forum) before you visit.

## A few things we look after together

- Return shared tools clean and dry. Ask before borrowing anything unfamiliar.
- Leave the paths clear and tell someone about a broken tool or loose step.
- Ask before taking photographs of other people.
- Share knowledge kindly. Everyone was a beginner once.
- Make room for different abilities, schedules, and ways of taking part.

## Keep the notebook growing

Add a planting tip, a repair guide, or a note about something that worked.
The [journal](/blog) holds longer stories; the [forum](/forum) is for questions.
Our [front page](/) is a wiki page too, cared for by the community.
